## How the plan progresses

- Double progression: when you reach the top of the rep range on every set with the target reps in reserve, add 2-5% load or move to the harder version.
- Move to the easier version if you miss the bottom of the range or feel pain of 4/10+.
- Weekly volume rises at most ~20%; a lighter week comes when fatigue trends up or progress stalls.
